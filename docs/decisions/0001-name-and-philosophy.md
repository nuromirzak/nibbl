# 0001 Name and philosophy

Date: 2026-10-02. Status: accepted. Spec: [section 1](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-nibbl-design.md), [principles](../product-principles.md).

## Context
The product needs a short, ownable name and a filter for every later feature decision.

## Decision
- Name: **Nibbl**. A nibble is half a byte, and the pet nibbles bugs. Pets are "nibbls".
- Mission: **make waiting for Claude feel warm**.
- Philosophy: **quiet, honest, yours**, expanded into 5 principles: Honest, Quiet, Yours, Earned, Tiny.

## Why
- The name is short, searchable and says what the pet does.
- The five principles give a cheap veto: a feature that nags, costs tokens without opt-in, or fakes an emotion is out.

## Rejected alternatives
- Pixling, Bitling: weaker fit with the "bugs" story.
- Termling: already taken.
- Any "-gotchi" name: Bandai trademark risk.

## Consequences
- Every spec section is checked against the five principles.
- Branding stays original (see 0008 for art risk).
