A tiny pixel pet that nibbles your bugs.

Nibbl is a Claude Code mod. Your nibbl hatches after real work, lives above the prompt, and eats the bugs your tests squash. It never blocks, never nags, and costs zero tokens.

Luck gives the start

Every nibbl rolls a genome when it hatches: body, colors, pattern, eyes, ears. Effort gives the form later.

Shiny is rolled separately, so a shiny can come from any tier. Every nibbl has at least one trait rarer than 5%.
